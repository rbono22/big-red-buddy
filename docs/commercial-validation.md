# Price evidence and the first 50 customers

The proposed price is **$15 per student per month**, matching
`eval/pack/unit_economics.csv`. This is a proposed price, not current collected
revenue. At 50 paying customers it would produce $750 monthly gross revenue.
Profit and break-even remain unknown until complete operating costs are verified.

## Evidence as of November 20, 2026

The founder reported: “I had classmates tell me in person they would pay that
amount.” This supports qualitative interest at $15/month. The number of people,
conversation dates, exact questions and responses were not supplied. These are
not independently recorded survey results, paid orders or proof of retention.
No campus-license agreement or Student & Academic Affairs endorsement has been
established. Do not add a respondent count or conversion rate without records.

## Planned acquisition path

| Stage | Target new paying students | Route | Evidence needed to proceed |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| First cohort | 10 | Invite classmates who expressed interest to try the anonymous demo, then their own reviewed schedule. | Dated consented pilot signup, activation, explicit $15 offer and payment. |
| Peer referrals | 20 | Ask activated pilot students for introductions within the program. | Track referral source, qualified demos and paid conversions separately. |
| Program expansion | 20 | Student-club/course demos and a requested Student & Academic Affairs discussion about access or a campus license. | Organizer permission, recorded opt-ins and payment; a meeting is not a contract. |
| Total | 50 | Stage targets, not achieved customers. | Measure retention and weekly active use before increasing capacity. |

The timetable depends on actual conversion and capacity measurements. Do not
assume every invitation becomes a paying customer. A campus contract is an
alternative distribution route requiring a separate quote; its price is not
assumed to equal 50 individual subscriptions.

## Ready-to-use interview questions

Ask voluntarily after the person uses the demo; record a pseudonymous ID, date,
cohort and exact response in a private research file.

1. How do you currently collect deadlines, and how many minutes does it take?
2. What did you find useful or untrustworthy in the reviewed schedule?
3. Would you pay $15/month for this during the term: yes, no, or unsure? Why?
4. Would you opt into a pilot? Record this separately from willingness to pay.
5. After a week, how many minutes did it save on a specific task?

These questions are prepared for collection. No invitations or survey messages
were sent as part of this revision.
