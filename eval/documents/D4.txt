Product Manager

ABOUT BLACK KITE
Black Kite is the global leader in third-party cyber risk intelligence, trusted by more than 3,000 organizations worldwide. We give security and business leaders a
continuous, outside-in view of their entire vendor ecosystem — translating complex cyber, financial, and compliance signals into clear, actionable risk intelligence.

We go beyond open standards-based cyber ratings. Black Kite helps organizations make smarter risk decisions, strengthen business resilience, and scale their third-
party cyber risk management programs in an increasingly complex digital environment. Our work has earned consistent recognition from customers and industry
analysts alike.

WHY BLACK KITE
We're a fast-moving, high-impact team solving one of the most critical challenges in cybersecurity today. If you're looking to do meaningful work alongside sharp,
collaborative people — and grow your career in a space that matters — you're in the right place.

THE OPPORTUNITY

This is an exciting time for growth within our Product team! We are looking for a Product Manager to own some key innovation projects within our roadmap. In this
role, you will be at the center of making things happen. You'll work closely with (and report to) the Chief Product Officer, as well as partner cross-functionally with
engineering, marketing, and sales teams to support and contribute to strategy development, priorities, and use cases for Black Kite's platform.

You like to think strategically to define priorities. You are passionate about your customers’ problems and spend time empathizing with them. You are ambitious and

WHAT YOU'LL OWN
* Own the lifecycle of projects and work with the various development & marketing teams to bring solutions to our customers and internal stakeholders
* Continually collaborate in all aspects of Black Kite innovation including product ideation, strategy, vision, roadmapping, & execution
* Work with product architecture and design to build a vision for customer solutions
* Perform and analyze the market and strategies related to the product, including conducting pricing & competitive analysis
* Portrays the voice of the customer as a means to communicate ideas to foster innovation

WHAT YOU'LL BRING
* BA or BS Degree, as well as 3+ years experience as a technical product manager, product manager, integration engineer, sales engineer, or related field
* Evidence of strong leadership skills, ownership, and creativity with a strong interest in using AI tools
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills
* Great analytical and problem-solving skills
* Good organization and multitasking abilities
* Akeen eye for detail
* Ability to work in a fast-paced environment, to prioritize effectively, and to apply critical thinking and good judgment
+ Exceptional verbal and written communication skills with the ability to interact effectively with team members and customers at all levels
* Strong personal drive, passion, enthusiasm and ethics
* Understanding of the Cybersecurity industry, a plus
