Your knowledge source

The live app retrieves the student’s imported records—Canvas, syllabi, and Telegram records—with their source evidence. Selecting relevant records keeps unrelated material out of the answer and preserves the complete matching date list. Re-imports surface changed material; review is reopened when event details change. Freshness depends on the latest import, not on a model remembering an old document.
